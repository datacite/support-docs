DataCite provides different types of transfer services. Any organization wishing perform a transfer must read the instructions carefully and comply with all requirements before proceeding.

There are 3 main types of transfers requiring an intervention by DataCite:

- [Prefix transfers, DOI Distributed Administration and Aliasing (between RAs)](doc:prefix-transfers-and-aliasing)

- [Repository Transfers between DataCite Members](doc:repository-transfers)

- [DOI transfers between DataCite Members](doc:doi-transfers)

This policy refers to transfers requiring coordination by DataCite between Registration Agencies and Members/Consortia. Consortium leads, Consortium Organizations and Members can perform some transfers within their own accounts:

DataCite Consortium Leads can transfer Repository accounts between the different Consortium Organizations in the Consortium Structure.

- [Repository Transfers (Consortium Leads only)](doc:repository-transfer)

DataCite Direct Members, DataCite Consortium Leads and Consortium Organizations can transfer DOIs between their Repositories.

-  [Transfer DOIs Between Repositories](doc:fabrica-transfer-dois)
