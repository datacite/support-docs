The "Add to ORCID Record" feature in [DataCite Commons](https://commons.datacite.org/) allows a researcher to manually add a DataCite DOI to their [ORCID record](https://orcid.org/).

> 📘 DataCite Profiles
> 
> You need a [DataCite Profiles](https://profiles.datacite.org/) account to use this service. In order for the "Add to ORCID Record" function to work, the "Link Works to ORCID" setting in DataCite Profiles must be enabled.
> 
> You can also enable the "ORCID Auto-Update" setting to add works to your ORCID record automatically when your ORCID is included as a creator in a DataCite DOI metadata. **Keep in mind that "ORCID Auto-Update" must be enabled for your account to be eligible for a [DataCite trust marker](doc:why-dont-my-works-have-a-trust-marker-in-orcid-if-i-use-orcid-search-link).** Follow the instructions in the [Profiles documentation](doc:datacite-profiles-user-documentation).
> 
> Only DataCite DOIs can be claimed in DataCite Commons.

To add a DOI to your ORCID record in DataCite Commons:

1. First ** sign in ** by clicking the button at the top right of the page.
2. Search for a DOI using the search box.
3. Click to open the full record of the DOI.
4. Click on the **"Add to ORCID Record"** button in the sidebar.
5. An **"Adding to ORCID Record"** message will appear. The request may take a few seconds to complete. If the error "No user and/or ORCID token" appears, check that the ORCID token is enabled in [your account settings](doc:datacite-profiles-user-documentation).

Once complete, the DOI will appear in the associated ORCID record and the button will change to **"Added to ORCID Record"**. Click the **"Manage in ORCID"** button below to navigate to your ORCID record, where you can view added works and remove them if needed. Use the **"Re-Add"** button to add works to your ORCID record again if you removed them in error.

> 🚧 Researcher Profiles
> 
> Added works are sent to ORCID and appear in your ORCID record. They do not appear in the [DataCite Researcher Profile](doc:datacite-researcher-profiles). Only DOIs that have the ORCID iD included in the DOI metadata are linked to the DataCite Researcher Profile.

> 📘 Why is it different from the ORCID auto update?
> 
> Adding a DOI using [DataCite Commons](https://commons.datacite.org/) is useful when you want to claim a work that does not include your ORCID iD in the DOI metadata. You can also automatically add DOIs to your ORCID record that include your ORCID iD in the creator metadata using [ORCID auto-update](doc:how-to-activate-orcid-auto-update).
